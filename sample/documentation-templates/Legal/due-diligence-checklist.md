## Corporate Structure and Governance

- [ ] Certificate of incorporation / formation documents
- [ ] Bylaws or operating agreement
- [ ] Board and shareholder resolutions
- [ ] Ownership and cap table

## Material Contracts

- [ ] Customer contracts reviewed
- [ ] Vendor and supplier agreements reviewed
- [ ] Lease agreements reviewed
- [ ] Change of control provisions identified

## Intellectual Property

- [ ] Patents, trademarks, copyrights inventoried
- [ ] IP ownership confirmed
- [ ] Third-party licenses reviewed
- [ ] Open source usage reviewed

## Employment and Labor

- [ ] Employment agreements reviewed
- [ ] Non-compete and NDA agreements reviewed
- [ ] Benefits and equity plans reviewed
- [ ] Employee classifications verified

## Litigation and Disputes

- [ ] Active litigation identified
- [ ] Threatened claims documented
- [ ] Regulatory proceedings reviewed

## Regulatory and Compliance

- [ ] Applicable licenses and permits verified
- [ ] Industry-specific regulations reviewed
- [ ] Data privacy compliance assessed
