---

## Launch Overview

## Merchandising and Display

- [ ] Planogram finalized and approved
- [ ] Display fixtures ordered and installed
- [ ] Signage printed and placed
- [ ] Product positioned per visual standards

## Inventory and Stock Preparation

- [ ] Initial order quantity confirmed
- [ ] Stock received and checked in
- [ ] Back stock organized
- [ ] Replenishment thresholds set

## Pricing and Promotions

- [ ] Pricing confirmed in POS system
- [ ] Promotional pricing programmed
- [ ] Promo materials distributed

## Marketing and Communications

- [ ] Email/SMS campaigns scheduled
- [ ] Social media posts scheduled
- [ ] In-store signage live
- [ ] Website/app updated

## Staff Training

- [ ] Product knowledge session completed
- [ ] Selling points communicated
- [ ] FAQs distributed

## Launch Day Checklist

- [ ] Floor walk completed
- [ ] All displays stocked
- [ ] POS transactions tested
- [ ] Staff briefed
