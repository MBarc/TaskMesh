# New Hire Laptop Setup

## Welcome

Welcome to the team! This document walks you through setting up your company-issued laptop on your first day.

## What's in the Box

- Dell Latitude 5540 or MacBook Pro 14"
- Power adapter and USB-C dock
- Wireless mouse and keyboard
- Asset tag sticker (already applied to the bottom of the device)

## Initial Boot

1. Power on the laptop and connect to the **CorpSetup** Wi-Fi network (password: `Welcome2025!`)
2. Sign in with the temporary credentials from your onboarding email
3. The device will auto-enroll in **Microsoft Intune** and begin downloading policies

## Required Software

The following software will be pushed automatically via Intune within 30 minutes of enrollment:

- Microsoft 365 Suite (Outlook, Word, Excel, PowerPoint, Teams)
- GlobalProtect VPN Client
- CrowdStrike Falcon (endpoint protection)
- Slack Desktop
- Google Chrome
- Adobe Acrobat Reader

### Optional Software

Request these through the IT Self-Service Portal if needed:

- Visual Studio Code
- Docker Desktop
- Postman
- Figma Desktop
- Zoom

## Email and Calendar

1. Open **Outlook** and sign in with your `firstname.lastname@example.com` credentials
2. Your mailbox and calendar will sync automatically
3. Shared team calendars can be added via **File > Open & Export > Open Shared Calendar**

## Printer Setup

Printers are auto-discovered on the corporate network. To add one manually:

```
Settings > Printers & Scanners > Add Printer
```

Common printers:

| Floor | Printer Name | Location |
|-------|-------------|----------|
| 1st | `PRN-LOBBY-01` | Near reception |
| 2nd | `PRN-ENG-02` | Engineering area |
| 3rd | `PRN-HR-03` | HR conference room |

## Security Reminders

- **Never** share your password with anyone, including IT staff
- Lock your laptop when stepping away (`Win + L` or `Cmd + Ctrl + Q`)
- Report any suspicious emails to `phishing@example.com`
- Encrypt any USB drives before transferring sensitive data
