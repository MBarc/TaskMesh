# Social Media Playbook

**Document Owner:** Social Media Team
**Last Updated:** February 2024
**Version:** 2.0

## Overview

This playbook provides guidelines for managing our social media presence across all platforms.

## Platform Strategy

### LinkedIn (Primary B2B)
- **Audience:** Decision-makers, professionals, job seekers
- **Content Mix:** 40% thought leadership, 30% company updates, 20% culture, 10% product
- **Posting Frequency:** 1-2 posts per day
- **Best Times:** Tue-Thu, 8-10am and 12-1pm ET
- **Hashtags:** 3-5 relevant hashtags

### Twitter/X
- **Audience:** Tech community, industry influencers, customers
- **Content Mix:** 50% engagement/conversation, 30% news/insights, 20% company
- **Posting Frequency:** 3-5 tweets per day
- **Best Times:** Weekdays 9am-3pm ET
- **Hashtags:** 1-2 hashtags maximum

### Instagram
- **Audience:** Potential employees, culture followers, visual learners
- **Content Mix:** 50% culture, 30% behind-the-scenes, 20% product visuals
- **Posting Frequency:** 4-5 feed posts per week, daily stories
- **Best Times:** Mon-Fri 11am-1pm, 7-9pm ET
- **Hashtags:** 5-10 hashtags (in comments)

### YouTube
- **Audience:** Technical users, product evaluators, visual learners
- **Content Mix:** 40% tutorials, 30% webinars, 20% customer stories, 10% company
- **Posting Frequency:** 2-4 videos per month
- **Best Times:** Thursday or Friday, 12-3pm ET

## Content Guidelines

### Voice by Platform

| Platform | Tone | Examples |
|----------|------|----------|
| LinkedIn | Professional, insightful | "Here's what we learned from analyzing..." |
| Twitter | Conversational, witty | "Hot take: spreadsheets aren't a CRM" |
| Instagram | Friendly, authentic | "Meet the team behind the magic ✨" |

### Post Structure

**LinkedIn:**
```
Hook (first 140 characters)

Main content (insights, story, or value)

Call to action

#Hashtags
```

**Twitter:**
```
Hook or statement
Context or insight
CTA or question
Link (if applicable)
```

### Visual Guidelines
- Use branded templates for quotes and stats
- Images: 1200x627 (LinkedIn/Twitter), 1080x1080 (Instagram)
- Videos: Under 2 minutes for feed, under 60 seconds for stories
- Include captions on all videos

## Engagement Guidelines

### Response Times

| Platform | Expected Response |
|----------|-------------------|
| Twitter | Within 2 hours (business hours) |
| LinkedIn | Within 4 hours |
| Instagram | Within 24 hours |

### Response Protocol

**Positive Comments:**
- Like/heart within 1 hour
- Reply with thanks and additional value
- Share particularly good ones internally

**Questions:**
- Answer directly if possible
- Direct to support for technical issues
- Tag relevant team members when needed

**Negative Comments:**
- Don't delete (unless violates policy)
- Respond professionally and helpfully
- Take detailed discussions to DM
- Escalate to manager if serious

**Spam/Violations:**
- Hide or delete
- Block repeat offenders
- Document patterns

### Comments We Don't Respond To
- Trolling with no legitimate concern
- Profanity or harassment
- Unrelated self-promotion
- Obvious bot activity

## Crisis Management

### Social Media Crisis Defined
- Viral negative content about company
- Customer data breach
- Executive misconduct allegations
- Product failure affecting many customers

### Crisis Response Steps

1. **Pause** scheduled posts immediately
2. **Alert** marketing leadership and PR
3. **Monitor** all mentions closely
4. **Wait** for approved response (don't improvise)
5. **Respond** with approved messaging only
6. **Document** everything

### Escalation Contacts
- Social Media Manager: Direct line
- PR Director: For media inquiries
- Legal: For liability concerns
- Executive team: For major crises
