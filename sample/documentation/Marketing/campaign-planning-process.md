# Campaign Planning Process

**Document Owner:** Marketing Operations
**Last Updated:** February 2024
**Version:** 1.5

## Overview

This document outlines the end-to-end process for planning, executing, and measuring marketing campaigns.

## Campaign Types

| Type | Duration | Budget Range | Lead Time |
|------|----------|--------------|-----------|
| Product Launch | 4-8 weeks | $50K-$150K | 8 weeks |
| Brand Awareness | Ongoing | $10K-$30K/month | 4 weeks |
| Demand Gen | 4-12 weeks | $20K-$75K | 6 weeks |
| Event Promotion | 6-8 weeks | $15K-$50K | 8 weeks |
| Customer Marketing | 2-4 weeks | $5K-$20K | 3 weeks |

## Planning Phase

### Step 1: Campaign Brief (Week 1)

Complete the following:

**Campaign Overview**
- Campaign name
- Type and category
- Start and end dates
- Total budget

**Objectives**
- Primary goal (specific and measurable)
- Secondary goals
- Key results (OKRs)

**Target Audience**
- Primary persona
- Secondary persona
- Geographic focus
- Industry focus

**Messaging**
- Key message (1 sentence)
- Supporting messages (3-5 points)
- Differentiation from competitors

**Channels**
- Primary channels
- Secondary channels
- Budget allocation by channel

### Step 2: Stakeholder Alignment (Week 1-2)

| Meeting | Attendees | Purpose |
|---------|-----------|---------|
| Kickoff | All stakeholders | Align on goals and scope |
| Creative brief | Creative team | Define visual direction |
| Channel review | Channel owners | Confirm capacity and approach |
| Final sign-off | Leadership | Approve budget and timeline |

### Step 3: Asset Development (Week 2-4)

**Creative Assets**
- [ ] Campaign theme/visual identity
- [ ] Hero imagery/video
- [ ] Social media graphics
- [ ] Email templates
- [ ] Landing page design
- [ ] Ad creative (all sizes)

**Content Assets**
- [ ] Landing page copy
- [ ] Email copy (all sends)
- [ ] Ad copy (all variants)
- [ ] Social media posts
- [ ] Blog posts (if applicable)
- [ ] Sales enablement materials

### Step 4: Technical Setup (Week 3-4)

- [ ] Landing page built and tested
- [ ] Form and CRM integration verified
- [ ] Tracking pixels installed
- [ ] UTM parameters defined
- [ ] Ad accounts configured
- [ ] Email automation set up
- [ ] Reporting dashboard created

## Execution Phase

### Pre-Launch Checklist (Week 4-5)

**Quality Assurance**
- [ ] All links tested
- [ ] Mobile responsiveness verified
- [ ] Form submissions tested
- [ ] Tracking confirmed in analytics
- [ ] Spelling and grammar checked
- [ ] Brand compliance verified
- [ ] Legal/compliance review (if needed)

**Team Readiness**
- [ ] Sales team briefed
- [ ] Support team informed
- [ ] Executive notification sent
- [ ] Go-live meeting scheduled

### Ongoing Management

**Daily**
- Monitor ad performance
- Check for issues/errors
- Respond to engagement

**Weekly**
- Performance review meeting
- Optimization adjustments
- Budget pacing check
- Content refresh (if needed)

## Measurement Framework

### Funnel Metrics

```
Impressions → Clicks → Visits → Leads → MQLs → SQLs → Opportunities → Closed Won
```

### Metric Definitions

| Metric | Definition | Target |
|--------|------------|--------|
| Impressions | Total ad/content views | Varies |
| CTR | Clicks / Impressions | >1% (ads) |
| Conversion rate | Leads / Visits | >5% |
| CPL | Spend / Leads | <$50 |
| MQL rate | MQLs / Leads | >25% |
| Pipeline generated | $ value created | Campaign-specific |
| ROI | Revenue / Spend | >3x |

### Attribution Model
- First-touch for awareness campaigns
- Multi-touch for demand gen
- Last-touch for bottom-funnel

## Reporting

### Weekly Report (During Campaign)
- Spend vs. budget
- Key metrics vs. targets
- Top performing content/channels
- Issues and optimizations made

### Final Report (Post-Campaign)

**Executive Summary**
- Goals vs. results
- Key wins and learnings
- ROI calculation

**Detailed Analysis**
- Channel performance breakdown
- Audience insights
- Content performance
- Conversion funnel analysis

**Recommendations**
- What to repeat
- What to change
- Ideas for future campaigns

### Post-Campaign Review Meeting
- Schedule within 2 weeks of campaign end
- All stakeholders attend
- Document learnings in campaign library
