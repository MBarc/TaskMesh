# Brand Guidelines

**Document Owner:** Creative Team
**Last Updated:** February 2024
**Version:** 3.0

## Brand Overview

### Mission Statement
Empowering businesses to achieve more through innovative technology solutions.

### Brand Personality
- **Innovative:** We push boundaries and embrace new ideas
- **Trustworthy:** We're reliable partners our customers can count on
- **Approachable:** We make complex technology accessible
- **Bold:** We're confident in our vision and capabilities

### Brand Voice
- Clear and concise
- Professional but friendly
- Confident, not arrogant
- Helpful and educational

## Visual Identity

### Logo

#### Primary Logo
- Use on white or light backgrounds
- Minimum size: 120px wide (digital), 1 inch (print)
- Clear space: Height of the "T" on all sides

#### Logo Variations
| Version | Use Case |
|---------|----------|
| Full color | Default for most applications |
| White/reversed | Dark backgrounds |
| Icon only | Small spaces, favicons |
| Monochrome | Single-color printing |

#### Logo Don'ts
- Don't rotate or skew
- Don't change colors
- Don't add effects (shadows, gradients)
- Don't place on busy backgrounds
- Don't stretch or distort

#### Accessibility
- All color combinations must meet WCAG 2.1 AA standards
- Minimum contrast ratio: 4.5:1 for body text
- Minimum contrast ratio: 3:1 for large text

### Photography Style

- Authentic, not overly staged
- Diverse representation
- Natural lighting preferred
- Technology in context of human use
- Avoid stock photo clichés

### Iconography

- Line style icons (2px stroke)
- Rounded corners
- Consistent sizing (24px grid)
- Use primary or slate colors only

## Tone of Voice

### Writing Principles

1. **Be Clear**
   - Use simple language
   - Avoid jargon unless necessary
   - Get to the point quickly

2. **Be Helpful**
   - Focus on user benefits
   - Provide actionable information
   - Anticipate questions

3. **Be Human**
   - Use conversational tone
   - Show personality
   - Admit mistakes when they happen

### Examples

| Instead of... | Write... |
|---------------|----------|
| "Leverage synergies" | "Work better together" |
| "Best-in-class solution" | "A tool that helps you..." |
| "Please do not hesitate to contact us" | "Questions? We're here to help" |
