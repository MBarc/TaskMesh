# Territory Planning Guide

**Document Owner:** Sales Operations
**Last Updated:** February 2024
**Version:** 2.0

## Overview

Effective territory planning maximizes revenue potential while ensuring fair distribution of opportunities across the sales team.

## Territory Strategy

### Territory Types

| Type | Description | Typical AE Count |
|------|-------------|------------------|
| Geographic | By region/state | 1-2 per region |
| Named Accounts | Strategic accounts | 10-25 accounts |
| Industry Vertical | By sector | 50-100 accounts |
| Company Size | By employee count | 100+ accounts |

### Current Structure

| Territory | Criteria | Rep Assignment |
|-----------|----------|----------------|
| Enterprise | >5,000 employees | Named accounts |
| Mid-Market | 500-5,000 employees | Geographic |
| SMB | 100-500 employees | Geographic + Industry |

## Planning Process

### Annual Territory Review (Q4)

**Timeline:**

| Week | Activity |
|------|----------|
| 1-2 | Data analysis and modeling |
| 3-4 | Draft territory maps |
| 5-6 | Manager review and input |
| 7-8 | Leadership approval |
| 9-10 | Rep communication |
| 11-12 | Implementation and training |

### Territory Design Principles

1. **Fair Opportunity:** Equal revenue potential
2. **Manageable Size:** Accounts can be properly covered
3. **Logical Grouping:** Geographic or industry clusters
4. **Growth Potential:** Mix of existing and whitespace
5. **Stability:** Minimize year-over-year changes

## Account Planning

### Strategic Account Plan Template

**Account Overview:**
- Company profile
- Key contacts and org chart
- Current relationship status
- Revenue history and potential

**Business Context:**
- Industry trends
- Company priorities
- Competitive landscape
- Technology environment

**Opportunity Assessment:**
- Identified opportunities
- Whitespace analysis
- Expansion potential
- Risk factors

**Action Plan:**
- 90-day priorities
- Key meetings to secure
- Resources needed
- Success metrics

### Account Tiering

| Tier | Definition | Coverage Model | Reviews |
|------|------------|----------------|---------|
| Tier 1 | Strategic, >$100K potential | Full account plan, exec sponsor | Quarterly |
| Tier 2 | Growth, $50-100K potential | Standard account plan | Bi-annual |
| Tier 3 | Maintain, <$50K potential | Basic tracking | Annual |

## Pipeline Management

### Pipeline Requirements

| Timeframe | Minimum Coverage |
|-----------|------------------|
| Current Quarter | 2.5x remaining quota |
| Next Quarter | 2x quota |
| Quarter +2 | 1x quota |

### Pipeline Quality

**Healthy Pipeline Indicators:**
- Balanced stage distribution
- Mix of deal sizes
- Various close dates
- Multiple opportunities per account

**Warning Signs:**
- Stale opportunities (>30 days no activity)
- Concentrated close dates
- Single large deals
- Deals pushed multiple times

## Territory Reviews

### Monthly Territory Review

**Agenda:**
1. Pipeline and forecast update
2. Account activity review
3. Blockers and support needed
4. Territory adjustments (if needed)

### Quarterly Business Review

**Components:**
- Territory performance vs. plan
- Account wins and losses
- Market dynamics and changes
- Next quarter strategy
- Resource requests

## Conflict Resolution

### Common Conflicts

| Scenario | Resolution |
|----------|------------|
| Account moves locations | Rep who had relationship keeps |
| Merger/acquisition | Case-by-case, sales ops decides |
| Inbound from wrong territory | Proper rep gets lead |
| Channel overlap | Pre-defined rules apply |
| Historic relationship | Sales ops mediates |

### Escalation Path
1. Reps discuss directly
2. Managers collaborate
3. Sales ops mediates
4. VP Sales decides (final)

A: Major changes annually; minor adjustments quarterly with approval.

**Q: What about acquired accounts?**
