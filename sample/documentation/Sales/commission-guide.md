# Commission Guide

**Document Owner:** Sales Compensation
**Last Updated:** February 2024
**Version:** 2024-1
**Classification:** Internal - Sales Only

## Overview

This guide explains how your compensation works, including base salary, commission structure, and special incentives.

## Compensation Philosophy

### Our Approach
- Competitive total compensation
- Reward performance and results
- Alignment with company goals
- Transparent and fair administration

### Principles
1. Pay tied to measurable results
2. Accelerators reward over-performance
3. On-target earnings (OTE) achievable
4. Timely and accurate payments

## Crediting Rules

### What Earns Commission

| Activity | Earns Commission | Notes |
|----------|------------------|-------|
| New customer closed | Yes | Full rate |
| Expansion deal | Yes | Expansion rate |
| Renewal | Yes | Renewal rate |
| Multi-year prepay | Yes | Full value at close |
| Month-to-month | Yes | Booked monthly |
| Professional services | No | Not commissioned |
| One-time fees | Varies | 50% rate |

### Revenue Recognition

| Payment Type | When Credited |
|--------------|---------------|
| Annual prepay | At contract signature |
| Quarterly prepay | At each payment |
| Monthly | At each payment |
| Multi-year | Year 1 at signature; Years 2+ at anniversary |

### Deal Splits

| Scenario | Split |
|----------|-------|
| Overlay specialist | Defined by role (typically 10-30%) |
| Territory transition | 50/50 if deal started by prior rep |
| Manager assistance | No split unless documented |
| Channel/partner | Rep gets full credit, partner gets margin |

## Quota Management

### Quota Setting
- Set annually in Q4 for following year
- Based on territory potential
- Approved by VP Sales and Finance
- Communicated before fiscal year start

### Quota Adjustments

| Scenario | Adjustment |
|----------|------------|
| Territory change | Pro-rated |
| Leave of absence | Pro-rated |
| New hire ramp | 50% Q1, 75% Q2, 100% Q3+ |
| Promotion | New quota upon promotion |

### Ramp for New Hires

| Month | Quota % | Commission % |
|-------|---------|--------------|
| Month 1-2 | 0% | Draw only |
| Month 3 | 50% | 100% rate |
| Month 4 | 75% | 100% rate |
| Month 5+ | 100% | 100% rate |

## Payment Process

### Timeline

| Event | Timing |
|-------|--------|
| Month close | 5th of following month |
| Commission calculation | 7th of following month |
| Commission statement | 10th of following month |
| Dispute deadline | 15th of following month |
| Payment | 25th of following month |

### Payment Method
- Paid through regular payroll
- Subject to standard deductions
- Visible on pay stub

### Disputes

**To dispute a commission:**
1. Review statement carefully
2. Gather supporting documentation
3. Submit via #commission-disputes by deadline
4. Resolution within 10 business days

## Clawbacks

### Clawback Scenarios

| Scenario | Clawback |
|----------|----------|
| Customer cancels within 90 days | 100% of commission |
| Customer cancels 91-180 days | 50% of commission |
| Materially misstated deal | Full commission |
| Fraudulent activity | Full commission + discipline |

### Protection
- No clawback after 180 days
- Downgrades don't trigger clawback
- Non-payment issues handled separately
