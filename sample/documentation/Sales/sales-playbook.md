2. **Consultative Approach:** Understand before you propose
3. **Long-term Relationships:** Value > transaction
4. **Integrity Always:** Do what's right for the customer

## Ideal Customer Profile (ICP)

### Primary Target

| Attribute | Criteria |
|-----------|----------|
| Company Size | 100-5,000 employees |
| Revenue | $10M - $500M |
| Industry | Technology, Financial Services, Healthcare |
| Geography | North America, UK, EU |
| Tech Stack | Modern cloud infrastructure |
| Pain Points | Manual processes, data silos, scaling challenges |

### Buyer Personas

**Primary: VP/Director of Operations**
- Goals: Efficiency, cost reduction, visibility
- Challenges: Too many tools, manual processes
- Motivators: ROI, team productivity
- Objections: Change management, integration

**Secondary: CTO/VP Engineering**
- Goals: Scalability, security, developer experience
- Challenges: Technical debt, resource constraints
- Motivators: Modern architecture, API-first
- Objections: Build vs. buy, security

**Influencer: End Users**
- Goals: Easy-to-use tools, less busywork
- Challenges: Too many systems to learn
- Motivators: Simplicity, time savings
- Objections: Learning curve

## Sales Process

### Stage Overview

| Stage | Entry Criteria | Exit Criteria | Probability |
|-------|----------------|---------------|-------------|
| Lead | Form fill, referral | Qualified fit | 5% |
| Discovery | Confirmed ICP fit | Pain identified | 15% |
| Demo | Stakeholder engaged | Solution fit confirmed | 30% |
| Proposal | Requirements defined | Proposal delivered | 50% |
| Negotiation | Proposal reviewed | Terms agreed | 75% |
| Closed Won | Contract signed | - | 100% |

### Stage Details

**1. Lead Qualification (1-3 days)**
- Verify ICP fit
- Confirm contact information
- Research company/industry
- Initial outreach

**2. Discovery (1-2 weeks)**
- Understand business context
- Identify pain points
- Map stakeholders
- Quantify impact of problem

**3. Demo (1-2 weeks)**
- Tailor presentation to needs
- Show relevant use cases
- Address technical questions
- Identify next steps

**4. Proposal (1-2 weeks)**
- Build business case
- Present pricing
- Address objections
- Involve decision makers

**5. Negotiation (1-3 weeks)**
- Finalize terms
- Legal review
- Procurement process
- Executive alignment

**6. Close**
- Contract execution
- Handoff to implementation
- Celebrate!

## Discovery Framework

### BANT+ Qualification

| Element | Questions |
|---------|-----------|
| **B**udget | Is there budget allocated? What's the approval process? |
| **A**uthority | Who makes the final decision? Who influences it? |
| **N**eed | What problem are you solving? What's the impact? |
| **T**imeline | When do you need to decide? What's driving the timeline? |
| **+** Competition | What other options are you considering? |

### Discovery Questions

**Situation:**
- Tell me about your current process for [X]
- What tools are you using today?
- How large is your team?

**Problem:**
- What's your biggest challenge with [X]?
- How is this impacting your business?
- What have you tried so far?

**Implication:**
- What happens if you don't solve this?
- How does this affect other parts of the business?
- What's the cost of the current approach?

**Need-Payoff:**
- If you could solve this, what would change?
- How would you measure success?
- What would your team do with the time saved?

## Objection Handling

### Common Objections

| Objection | Response |
|-----------|----------|
| "Too expensive" | Focus on TCO and ROI; what's the cost of not solving? |
| "We're not ready" | Understand timeline; what would make you ready? |
| "We'll build it ourselves" | Compare time/cost; focus on core competency |
| "Competitor is cheaper" | Differentiate on value; TCO analysis |
| "Need to think about it" | Clarify concerns; what information would help? |
| "Happy with current solution" | Uncover pain; what would make you consider change? |

### Objection Handling Framework

1. **Listen** - Let them finish, take notes
2. **Acknowledge** - "I understand that concern..."
3. **Clarify** - "Can you tell me more about...?"
4. **Respond** - Address with relevant proof points
5. **Confirm** - "Does that address your concern?"
