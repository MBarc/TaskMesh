## Overview

This guide outlines our pricing structure, packaging options, and discount guidelines.

## Pricing Philosophy

### Our Approach
- Value-based pricing aligned with customer outcomes
- Simple, transparent packaging
- Flexibility for enterprise needs
- Competitive positioning in market

### Pricing Principles
1. Price reflects value delivered
2. Clear differentiation between tiers
3. Encourage annual commitments
4. Protect deal economics

## Quoting Process

### Quote Components

1. **Products & Quantity**
   - SKU and description
   - User count or quantity
   - Term (monthly/annual)

2. **Pricing**
   - List price
   - Discount (if applicable)
   - Net price

3. **Terms**
   - Start date
   - End date
   - Payment terms
   - Auto-renewal clause

4. **Signatures**
   - Customer signature
   - Company signature
   - Date

### Quote Validity
- Standard: 30 days
- Extended (with approval): 60 days
- After expiration: Re-quote required

## Negotiation Strategies

### Value-Based Selling

**Instead of discounting, add value:**
- Additional training sessions
- Extended implementation support
- Premium support tier upgrade
- Additional storage or users

### Protecting Margins

| When Customer Says | Response Strategy |
|-------------------|-------------------|
| "Competitor is cheaper" | Focus on TCO, differentiate on value |
| "Need 40% off" | Ask what they value; trade for term/commitment |
| "Budget is fixed" | Reduce scope to fit budget |
| "Need discount to close" | Ask for something in return |

### Trade-offs

| Customer Wants | We Can Ask For |
|----------------|----------------|
| Higher discount | Multi-year term |
| Extended payment terms | Larger commitment |
| Additional users | Annual payment |
| Custom features | Premium tier |

## Renewals

### Renewal Pricing

| Scenario | Price Change |
|----------|--------------|
| Standard renewal | Up to 5% increase |
| Uplift (new licenses) | Current list price |
| Multi-year signed | Locked rate |
| At-risk account | Manager discretion |

### Renewal Discounts
- Maintain original discount level
- No additional discounts without approval
- Lost discounts don't roll forward

## Special Situations

### Channel/Partner Pricing
- Partner discount: 20% off list
- Partner can add margin
- Same discount approval rules apply

### Pilot/POC Pricing
- 30-day free pilot available
- Extended pilot: 50% discount
- Full conversion required at end

### Contract Modifications
- Mid-term adds: Pro-rated
- Mid-term reduces: Not permitted
- Tier upgrades: Immediate, pro-rated
- Tier downgrades: At renewal only
