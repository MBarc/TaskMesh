# Benefits Overview

**Document Owner:** Benefits Team
**Last Updated:** February 2024

## Our Philosophy

We believe in providing comprehensive benefits that support our employees' health, financial security, and overall well-being.

## Health & Wellness

### Medical Insurance

**Plan Options:**

| Plan | Premium (Employee) | Deductible | Out-of-Pocket Max |
|------|-------------------|------------|-------------------|
| PPO | $150/month | $500 | $3,000 |
| HDHP | $75/month | $1,500 | $4,500 |
| PPO Family | $450/month | $1,000 | $6,000 |
| HDHP Family | $225/month | $3,000 | $9,000 |

**Coverage Highlights:**
- Preventive care: 100% covered
- Prescription drugs: Tiered copays
- Mental health: Covered same as medical
- Telehealth: $0 copay

### Dental Insurance

| Tier | Coverage |
|------|----------|
| Preventive | 100% |
| Basic | 80% |
| Major | 50% |
| Orthodontia | 50% (up to $2,000 lifetime) |
| Annual Maximum | $2,000 |

**Premium:** $25/month (employee), $75/month (family)

### Vision Insurance

| Benefit | Coverage |
|---------|----------|
| Eye exam | $10 copay |
| Frames | $150 allowance |
| Lenses | 100% covered |
| Contacts | $150 allowance |

**Premium:** $10/month (employee), $25/month (family)

- **Gym Subsidy:** $50/month reimbursement

- **Health Coaching:** 6 sessions/year included

### Mental Health

- **EAP:** 8 free counseling sessions/year

- **Meditation App:** Free subscription
- **Mental Health Days:** Included in sick leave
- **Support Groups:** Various ERG-sponsored
