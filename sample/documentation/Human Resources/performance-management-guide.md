# Performance Management Guide

**Document Owner:** HR Business Partners
**Last Updated:** February 2024
**Version:** 2.8

## Overview

This guide outlines our performance management philosophy, processes, and best practices for managers and employees.

## Philosophy

### Our Approach
- Continuous feedback over annual reviews
- Focus on growth and development
- Fair and consistent evaluation
- Clear expectations and accountability

### Performance Principles
1. Set clear, measurable goals
2. Provide regular feedback
3. Develop talent continuously
4. Recognize and reward achievement
5. Address issues promptly

## Goal Setting

### SMART Goals Framework
- **S**pecific: Clear and well-defined
- **M**easurable: Quantifiable outcomes
- **A**chievable: Realistic given resources
- **R**elevant: Aligned with team/company goals
- **T**ime-bound: Clear deadlines

### Goal Categories

| Category | Description | Weighting |
|----------|-------------|-----------|
| Business Impact | Revenue, efficiency, customer outcomes | 50-60% |
| Role Excellence | Core job responsibilities | 20-30% |
| Development | Skills growth, learning | 10-20% |

### Goal Examples

**Good:**
"Increase customer retention rate from 85% to 90% by Q4 through implementing proactive outreach program"

**Needs Improvement:**
"Improve customer satisfaction" (not specific or measurable)

## Continuous Feedback

### 1:1 Meetings
- Frequency: Weekly or bi-weekly
- Duration: 30-60 minutes
- Owned by: Employee (agenda), Manager (guidance)

### 1:1 Topics
- Progress on goals
- Roadblocks and support needed
- Career development
- Feedback (both directions)
- Recognition

### Feedback Best Practices

**Giving Feedback:**
- Timely: As close to the event as possible
- Specific: Concrete examples
- Balanced: Strengths and growth areas
- Actionable: Clear next steps

**Receiving Feedback:**
- Listen fully
- Ask clarifying questions
- Thank the giver
- Reflect and act

## Performance Reviews

### Review Components

| Component | Description | Who Provides |
|-----------|-------------|--------------|
| Self-Assessment | Employee's view of performance | Employee |
| Manager Assessment | Evaluation against goals | Manager |
| Peer Feedback | 360 input from colleagues | Selected peers |
| Calibration | Cross-team consistency | Leadership |

### Rating Scale

| Rating | Definition | Distribution |
|--------|------------|--------------|
| Exceptional | Far exceeds expectations | ~10% |
| Exceeds | Consistently exceeds expectations | ~25% |
| Meets | Fully meets expectations | ~55% |
| Needs Improvement | Below expectations | ~8% |
| Unsatisfactory | Significantly below | ~2% |

### Writing Effective Reviews

**Structure:**
1. Overall summary (2-3 sentences)
2. Key accomplishments
3. Strengths demonstrated
4. Growth opportunities
5. Goals for next period

**Do:**
- Use specific examples
- Focus on behaviors and outcomes
- Be honest and constructive
- Document throughout the year

**Don't:**
- Surprise the employee
- Focus only on recent events
- Compare to other employees
- Use vague language

## Compensation

### Merit Increases

| Rating | Typical Increase |
|--------|------------------|
| Exceptional | 6-8% |
| Exceeds | 4-5% |
| Meets | 2-3% |
| Needs Improvement | 0% |

### Bonus Eligibility

| Rating | Bonus Multiplier |
|--------|------------------|
| Exceptional | 150% of target |
| Exceeds | 110% of target |
| Meets | 100% of target |
| Needs Improvement | 0% |

### Promotion Criteria
- Consistent "Exceeds" or above
- Demonstrating next-level skills
- Business need for the role
- Minimum time in role (typically 18 months)

## Performance Issues

### Performance Improvement Plan (PIP)

**When Used:**
- Pattern of underperformance
- Serious performance gap
- After coaching has not worked

**PIP Components:**
1. Clear statement of gap
2. Specific improvement required
3. Support and resources provided
4. Timeline (typically 30-60 days)
5. Consequences if not improved

**Manager Responsibilities:**
- Document everything
- Provide regular check-ins
- Offer genuine support
- Make fair assessment

## For Managers

### Key Responsibilities
- Set clear expectations
- Provide regular feedback
- Document performance
- Conduct fair reviews
- Develop your team
- Address issues promptly

### Manager Training
- New manager training (required)
- Giving feedback workshop
- Difficult conversations training
- Bias awareness training
