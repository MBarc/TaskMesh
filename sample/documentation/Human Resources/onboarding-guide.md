# New Employee Onboarding Guide

**Document Owner:** HR Onboarding Team
**Last Updated:** February 2024
**Version:** 3.2

## Welcome!

Congratulations on joining our team! This guide will help you navigate your first days, weeks, and months.

## Before Your First Day

### What We'll Send You
- Welcome email with login credentials
- Equipment (laptop, accessories)
- Company swag pack
- Paperwork links (complete before Day 1)

### What You Should Complete
- [ ] Accept calendar invites for orientation
- [ ] Complete I-9 and tax forms
- [ ] Set up direct deposit
- [ ] Review the employee handbook
- [ ] Upload your photo for our directory

## Your First Day

### Schedule

| Time | Activity | Location |
|------|----------|----------|
| 9:00 AM | Welcome & badge pickup | Reception |
| 9:30 AM | HR orientation | Conference Room A |
| 11:00 AM | IT setup & security training | Your desk |
| 12:00 PM | Lunch with your team | Team's choice |
| 1:30 PM | Meet with your manager | Manager's office |
| 3:00 PM | Office tour & introductions | Building-wide |
| 4:00 PM | Self-guided exploration | Your desk |

### What to Bring
- Government-issued ID (for I-9)
- Banking information (for direct deposit)
- Emergency contact information
- Any questions you have!

## Your First Week

### Day 1-2: Orientation
- Company history and culture
- Benefits enrollment
- Policy review
- System access and tools

### Day 3-4: Team Integration
- Team workflows and processes
- Key projects overview
- Stakeholder introductions
- Shadow a team member

### Day 5: Foundation Setting
- 1:1 with manager (goals discussion)
- Access all needed systems
- Join relevant Slack channels
- Set up recurring meetings

## Your First Month

### Week 1-2
- [ ] Complete all required training
- [ ] Set up 1:1s with key stakeholders
- [ ] Understand team OKRs
- [ ] Shadow key processes
- [ ] Join team rituals (standups, etc.)

### Week 3-4
- [ ] Take on first small project
- [ ] Provide onboarding feedback
- [ ] Complete 30-day check-in with manager
- [ ] Begin building relationships across teams

### 30-Day Milestones
- Completed all mandatory training
- Understand role expectations
- Productive contributor to team
- Building key relationships

## Your First 90 Days

### 30-60 Days
- Take ownership of projects
- Contribute ideas and feedback
- Deepen stakeholder relationships
- Identify areas for growth

### 60-90 Days
- Deliver measurable results
- Mentor newer team members
- Propose process improvements
- Complete 90-day review

### 90-Day Milestones
- Independently handling role responsibilities
- Positive impact on team outcomes
- Aligned on growth and development path
- Integrated into company culture
