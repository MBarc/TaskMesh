# Employee Handbook

**Document Owner:** Human Resources
**Last Updated:** February 2024

## Welcome

Welcome to our team! This handbook provides important information about our policies, benefits, and expectations.

## Company Overview

### Our Mission
Empowering businesses to achieve more through innovative technology solutions.

### Our Values
1. **Customer First** - We succeed when our customers succeed
2. **Innovation** - We embrace change and push boundaries
3. **Integrity** - We do the right thing, always
4. **Collaboration** - We're better together
5. **Excellence** - We strive for the highest quality

## Employment Policies

### Equal Employment Opportunity
We are committed to providing equal employment opportunities regardless of race, color, religion, sex, national origin, age, disability, genetic information, sexual orientation, gender identity, or veteran status.

### At-Will Employment
Employment with the company is "at-will." Either the employee or the company may terminate employment at any time, with or without cause or notice.

### Background Checks
All offers of employment are contingent upon successful completion of a background check.

## Work Schedule

### Standard Hours
- Core hours: 10:00 AM - 4:00 PM (local time)
- Standard week: 40 hours
- Flexibility available with manager approval

### Remote Work
- Hybrid model: 2-3 days in office recommended
- Fully remote positions available for some roles
- Equipment provided for home office setup
- VPN required for remote access

### Time Off

| Type | Amount | Accrual |
|------|--------|---------|
| PTO | 20 days | Accrued monthly |
| Sick Leave | 10 days | Available immediately |
| Holidays | 11 days | Fixed schedule |
| Parental Leave | 16 weeks | Upon birth/adoption |
| Bereavement | 5 days | As needed |

## Compensation

### Pay Schedule
- Bi-weekly (every other Friday)
- Direct deposit required
- Pay stubs available in HRIS

### Performance Reviews
- Annual reviews in Q1
- Mid-year check-ins in Q3
- Merit increases based on performance
- Promotion decisions made annually

### Overtime
- Non-exempt employees: 1.5x for hours over 40/week
- Overtime must be pre-approved
- Exempt employees: No overtime pay

## Benefits

### Health Insurance
- Medical, dental, vision coverage
- Company pays 80% of premiums
- Coverage begins first of month after hire
- Open enrollment annually in November

### Retirement
- 401(k) plan with company match
- 4% match (100% vested immediately)
- Roth option available
- Eligible after 30 days

### Additional Benefits
- Life insurance (2x salary)
- Short and long-term disability
- FSA/HSA options
- Employee assistance program
- Professional development budget ($2,000/year)
- Gym membership subsidy ($50/month)

## Code of Conduct

### Professional Behavior
- Treat all colleagues with respect
- Maintain confidentiality of company information
- Avoid conflicts of interest
- Report concerns through appropriate channels

### Communication
- Be responsive (within 24 hours for email)
- Use appropriate channels for communication
- Keep meetings purposeful and inclusive
- Provide constructive feedback

### Dress Code
- Business casual for office days
- Client-facing meetings: Business professional
- Remote: Appropriate for video calls

## Technology & Security

### Acceptable Use
- Company equipment is for business use
- Limited personal use acceptable
- No illegal activity
- No unauthorized software installation

### Data Protection
- Protect confidential information
- Use approved tools only
- Report security concerns immediately
- Complete annual security training

### Social Media
- Don't speak on behalf of company without approval
- Protect confidential information
- Be professional in public posts

## Workplace Safety

### Health & Safety
- Report all injuries immediately
- Follow emergency procedures
- Maintain a safe workspace
- Complete required safety training

### Harassment-Free Workplace
- Zero tolerance for harassment of any kind
- Report concerns to HR or anonymous hotline
- No retaliation for good-faith reports
- All reports investigated promptly

## Leaving the Company

### Resignation
- 2 weeks notice requested
- Exit interview scheduled
- Return all company property
- Final paycheck per state law

### Termination
- Involuntary separation handled professionally
- Severance eligibility varies by situation
- COBRA information provided
- References: Confirm dates and title only

## Questions?

Contact HR at hr@company.com or visit the HR portal.

*This handbook is not a contract. Policies may change at any time.*
