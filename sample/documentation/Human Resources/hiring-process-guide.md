# Hiring Process Guide

**Document Owner:** Talent Acquisition
**Last Updated:** February 2024
**Version:** 2.5

## Overview

This guide outlines our hiring process to ensure a consistent, fair, and efficient candidate experience.

## Hiring Philosophy

### Our Principles
- Hire for potential, not just experience
- Prioritize cultural add over cultural fit
- Make fair, unbiased decisions
- Treat every candidate with respect
- Move quickly but thoughtfully

### What We Look For
1. **Skills:** Can they do the job?
2. **Motivation:** Do they want this job?
3. **Values:** Do they align with our culture?
4. **Growth:** Will they grow with us?

## Opening a Role

### Step 1: Business Case

Before posting:
- [ ] Confirm headcount approval
- [ ] Define role scope and level
- [ ] Establish salary range
- [ ] Identify must-have vs. nice-to-have requirements
- [ ] Align with HR on timeline

### Step 2: Job Description

**Required Elements:**
- Role title and level
- Team and reporting structure
- Key responsibilities (5-7 bullets)
- Requirements (must-have skills)
- Nice-to-have qualifications
- Salary range (transparency)
- Benefits highlights
- Equal opportunity statement

**Writing Tips:**
- Use inclusive language
- Focus on outcomes, not credentials
- Limit years of experience requirements
- Avoid gendered language

### Step 3: Interview Plan

| Stage | Interviewer(s) | Duration | Focus |
|-------|----------------|----------|-------|
| Recruiter Screen | Recruiter | 30 min | Fit, interest, logistics |
| Hiring Manager | Hiring Manager | 45 min | Role fit, experience |
| Technical/Skills | Team members | 60 min | Technical ability |
| Culture/Values | Cross-functional | 45 min | Values alignment |
| Final | Skip-level | 30 min | Final assessment |

## Interview Process

### Recruiter Screen (30 min)

**Objectives:**
- Assess basic fit and interest
- Explain role and company
- Confirm logistics (compensation, timing)
- Evaluate communication skills

**Questions:**
- Tell me about your background
- Why are you interested in this role?
- What are your compensation expectations?
- What's your timeline?

### Hiring Manager Interview (45 min)

**Objectives:**
- Deep-dive on experience
- Assess role fit
- Evaluate leadership/growth potential
- Sell the opportunity

**Structure:**
- 5 min: Rapport building
- 25 min: Experience deep-dive
- 10 min: Role and team discussion
- 5 min: Candidate questions

### Technical/Skills Assessment (60 min)

**Options by Role:**

| Role Type | Assessment |
|-----------|------------|
| Engineering | Coding exercise + discussion |
| Design | Portfolio review + prompt |
| Sales | Role play + presentation |
| Marketing | Case study |
| Operations | Process improvement scenario |

**Guidelines:**
- Relate to actual job duties
- Allow reasonable time
- Provide clear instructions
- Evaluate consistently

### Culture/Values Interview (45 min)

**Objectives:**
- Assess values alignment
- Evaluate collaboration style
- Check for culture add

**Behavioral Questions:**
- Tell me about a time you received difficult feedback
- Describe a situation where you disagreed with a decision
- How do you approach working with people different from you?
- What does integrity mean to you?

## Making Decisions

### Debrief Meeting

**Agenda:**
1. Each interviewer shares assessment (2-3 min each)
2. Discuss concerns and strengths
3. Make hire/no-hire decision
4. Determine offer terms

**Decision Framework:**

| Assessment | Meaning | Action |
|------------|---------|--------|
| Strong Yes | Exceptional candidate | Move to offer |
| Yes | Meets bar | Move to offer |
| Lean Yes | Some concerns | Discuss further |
| Lean No | Significant concerns | Likely pass |
| No | Below bar | Pass |

### Avoiding Bias
- Use structured interviews
- Evaluate against criteria, not other candidates
- Check for "like me" bias
- Separate skill gaps from potential
- Document decisions

## Extending Offers

### Offer Process

1. Recruiter verbally extends offer
2. Candidate given 3-5 days to decide
3. Written offer sent
4. Signed offer returned
5. Background check initiated
6. Start date confirmed

### Offer Components
- Base salary
- Bonus target (if applicable)
- Equity (if applicable)
- Start date
- Reporting manager
- Position title

### Negotiation Guidelines
- Have clear range and flexibility
- Consider total compensation
- Maintain internal equity
- Document all negotiations
- Get approvals before committing
